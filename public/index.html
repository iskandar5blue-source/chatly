Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Creating an account and using every feature costs nothing.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so avoid sharing very sensitive information.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your phone browser and add it to your home screen, and it behaves like an app.

How do I install it on a computer?

Press a Download button at the top. It installs the app from your browser, with its own window and icon.
